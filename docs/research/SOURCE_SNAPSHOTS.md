## Source quality & rights boundaries

- A public GitHub commit verifies **file bytes at that revision**, not factual accuracy, religious interpretation, copyright ownership or personal publication consent.
- Historic character aliases stay within their originating story/version; real living people are not identified from fictional labels.
- Personal chat logs, full commercial music lyrics and unpublished artwork are outside public game content unless consent and rights exist.
- Novel dialogue, THE LISTENER and THE PERFECT RECORD remain explicitly proposed `GAME_CANON_CANDIDATE` content.
