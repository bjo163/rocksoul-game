## Visual identity guidelines
Faceless silhouettes, hoodies, grunge/manhwa or manga impact lines, playful typography, deliberate masks. A concealed face is a visual design preference, not permission to label anonymous real people.
- **SHADOW:** near-black silhouette, partial lunar tracing; emotionally restrained, not evil by default.
- **BATTERY:** angular dynamism, yellow electric effects; chaotic but not divine light.
- **WALTER:** white medical/repair layers, intentionally imperfect tools; no graphic mal-practice.
- **BABY-DOLL:** cardboard/doll or masked motif; psychological agency, no dehumanizing plot.
- **BLUE WITCH:** blue luminous mapping lines; magical fictional art only.
- **DOGS FROM UNIVERSE:** dynamic silhouette and theatrical move cues, no photograph of real person.
- **THE LISTENER:** customizable simple silhouette with accessible contrast.
