# ROCKSOUL GAME — The Eight-Era Story Spine
**All eight era names and their order are historical MOONWITNESS II anchors.** Events, playable sequences and endings below are proposed *game adaptations*, not recovered original episode transcripts.

## Core sequence
`CAPTURE → CONNECT → WITNESS → UNDERSTAND → PRESERVE → PROMOTE → MOVE FORWARD`.

Each act includes narrative stakes, a playable goal, NPC confrontation, a consequential choice, a restoration beat and a durable state change. An era is not a level count, calendar year, AGE CODE duration or spiritual station.

## ERA 00 — BEFORE LIGHT
Opening with MoonWitness's original SHADOW title, not a CGI creation-of-the-universe claim. Player finds "intentionally asked to be forgotten" in a damaged fictional log; chooses whether to annotate it with `UNKNOWN` rather than invent a name.

**Quest candidates:** `THE FIRST EMPTY FIELD`, `BEFORE THE MOON SPEAKS`. **Outcome:** a consent boundary saved in tutorial.

## ERA 01 — THE ARRIVAL
The player reaches SIGNAL STATION ZERO; BATTERY's impatience and WALTER's quick repairs accidentally scramble the transmitter. Player restores simple light and sound before receiving another fragment.

**Quest candidates:** `TICK / STATIC / HELLO?`, `POWER WITHOUT PROOF`. **First vertical slice:** one screen, generator, NPC, one restoration, save/load.

## ERA 02 — THE WITNESS
A failed physical clock and later replacement are the motif for memory, not verified temporal distortion. The player reads archive variants, solves a MoonRidley puzzle explicitly framed in the morning, and notices some missing audio is intentionally restricted.

**Quest candidates:** `TICK-TOCK`, `THE MORNING RIDLEY`, `THUNDERSTRUCK STATIC`. **Core mechanic:** compare `OBSERVATION`, `TESTIMONY`, `INTERPRETATION`, `UNKNOWN`.

## ERA 03 — THE RUNAWAYS
The crew scatters. DOGS tells impossible stories; BLUE WITCH discovers an alternate route. The player must listen without either accepting every spectacular claim or dismissing a witness wholesale.

**Quest candidates:** `WOE IS ME`, `LAMBRETA ROUTE`, `ELECTRIC FOREST`. **Core mechanic:** branching relationship graph without moral faith points.

## ERA 04 — THE EXPOSURE
THE PERFECT RECORD tries to erase unresolved contradictions. BABY-DOLL's plot twist reveals a withheld decision whose exact motivation is not yet author-approved. A mistaken public disclosure causes a tangible fictional consequence.

**Quest candidates:** `PATCH IT BRO!`, `CHAOS MEDIC`, `THE UNMERGED NAME`. **Core mechanic:** fix corrupted links; leave legitimate withheld story alone.

## ERA 05 — THE CHANGE
Two ZILL paths address privacy and reconciliation separately. Forgotten/HAVEN residents need actual support, not only a monument or “perfect account”. The player's restored resources have a social purpose.

**Quest candidates:** `ONE LIGHT AT HAVEN`, `LISTEN BEFORE YOU PATCH`, `THE SECOND CHANCE`. **Core mechanic:** opt-in community help and offline base repair.

## ERA 06 — END OF AN ERA
A choice to preserve what is justified, release permissioned work, or keep withheld evidence private. There is no `EXPOSE_ALL = TRUE_ENDING`. Difficulty comes from reconciling relationships and downstream impact.

**Finale candidates:** `THE LIVING ARCHIVE`, `WHAT DESERVES TO CONTINUE?`. **Core mechanic:** consequence ledger and alternate endings, no divine scoring.

## ERA 07 — NEXT PHASE
The world continues even when mysteries remain unresolved. Seasonal additions provide new stories with clear historical/game classification. Player creates their own responsible projects without being cast as cosmic savior.

**Season expansion:** AETHERION's fictional 10 realms/100 levels and themed 33 World Patches are optional content registers — NOT mechanically identical to the eight acts and NOT a promise that missing old documents are original-ready.
