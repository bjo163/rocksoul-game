# PART I — CORE VISION

## 1. The thesis

MOONWITNESS is the greater story/witness and knowledge universe; ROCKSOUL is the player's path through a fictional adaptation of it. The game is **not** the canonical religious research database, a replacement for original personal testimony, or a divine judgment simulator. It is a story in which *records can be broken, names can have aliases, memories can be misinterpreted, characters can want privacy, mistakes can be corrected, and people can act with compassion*.

The original MOONWITNESS II theme, **END OF AN ERA**, is the game's narrative spine. Its seven-step heritage `CAPTURE → CONNECT → WITNESS → UNDERSTAND → PRESERVE → PROMOTE → MOVE FORWARD` becomes the dramatic arc and gameplay reasoning. The source also distinguishes **PAST → AUDIT → EVALUATION → BALANCING → PROMOTING → NEXT PHASE**. Promoting is an act of deciding what deserves to continue, not advertising.

The ROCKSOUL moral loop preserves another historical instruction: **MENTOR → HELP → EXPERT → COMMUNICATE → LEARN → EXECUTE → DISTRIBUTE → REPEAT**. Help received should become help distributed. This makes the player useful rather than merely powerful.

### Central question

**How can a world devoted to preserving witnesses honor THE SHADOW, someone who intentionally asked to be forgotten?**

### Emotional promise

A good ending is not the completion of a perfect archive. It is an honest account of what happened, what cannot be known, who wants to be remembered or left in peace, and which choices help the living.

### Tone

Mysterious, cinematic, playful, emotionally sincere, satirical without humiliating believers or turning real people into targets; modern grunge/manga-line visual grammar, faceless silhouettes, hoodies, electric signal, vivid color. Dark atmosphere, **not** nihilism. Comedy can interrupt tension, but must not invalidate grief.

## 2. The single narrative hook — an adaptation proposal

In a city where nearly everything is linked to a chain of memories, a radio station still broadcasts music and static. A tiny clock no longer ticks. The archive begins receiving packets with missing origins: half a character name, a broken message, an unfinished promise and a request recorded in an unfamiliar voice: **“Don't remember me in the wrong way.”**

The player is not the chosen divine agent. The player is **THE LISTENER**, a new voluntary fictional role created for the game, one of many possible witnesses. The Listener is asked to restore the signal: not by forcing all fragments into one “truth,” but by inspecting relationships, asking characters, respecting private evidence and helping communities act on what is actually understood.

The signal loss starts as a technical mystery and becomes an ethical one. An abstract antagonist process — working title **THE PERFECT RECORD** — was designed, in this new fictional adaptation, to remove uncertainty from the archive. It merges similar names, cleans conflicting reports and deletes awkward gaps. Its promise is seductive: perfect order, no argument, no incomplete history. Its cost is catastrophic: it erases context, consent, agency and the difference between source, interpretation and fictional reconstruction.

**THE PERFECT RECORD IS A NEW GAME ANTAGONIST PROPOSAL, NOT AN EXISTING HISTORICAL MOONWITNESS CHARACTER.** Its opposition is methodological rather than ethnic, religious or supernatural. The last twist: some absences came from damage, while others came from someone's genuine wish not to be publicly exposed. The Listener must learn the difference.

## 3. The real stakes

- **External:** Lost/incorrectly merged records and signal routes damage places, identities, relationships and fictional community life.
- **Relational:** THE SHADOW is willing to remain hidden; BABY-DOLL's choice to forget may be self-protection rather than betrayal; crew members disagree over whether patching is always repairing.
- **Internal:** The Listener starts equating completeness with goodness, then learns to leave unknowns visible, obtain consent and take responsibility.
- **Societal:** The Forgotten / Haven asks why society archives legendary names while neglecting ordinary people.
- **Endgame:** Save a **living, imperfect constellation** of accounts, or impose a deceptively neat “one story to rule them all.” The preferred moral resolution is neither forced remembrance nor destructive erasure, but *consented and attributed preservation, honest uncertainty, practical care*.

Historical religious figures and actual traditions must not be deployed as playable enemies, boss monsters or divine impersonations.

## 6. The antagonist — proposed, not recovered

**THE PERFECT RECORD** is an aggregate-system antagonist in the GAME adaptation. It is not a god, religion, a living real group, or a claim about historical technology. It has a plausible motive: after witnessing too many contradictory reports, it tries to protect people from painful ambiguity by producing one clean definitive narrative. It invents equality where only similarity exists, assumes consent, changes dates, promotes rumors and cannot say “I don't know.”

Its final error is the opposite of the original MoonWitness laws: it cannot accept that a witness can be uncertain, an alias can remain distinct, or a person may retain legitimate privacy. THE LISTENER does not need to slay a monster; they must repair the *rules by which stories are handled* and ensure nobody gains unlimited authority over all histories.

This provides gameplay-compatible antagonism without casting any religion, ethnicity or person as the villain.

# PART III — THE WORLD AND EIGHT ERAS

## 7. Fictional world map (working game adaptation)

- **THE SIGNAL STATION** — starting zone; old clock, radio, empty channels, first puzzle. Inspired by ARSY/SIGNAL, Tick-Tock and Lost the Signal. Do not conflate fictional radio towers with the actual XV Fastify backend.
- **THE BASECAMP / HAVEN** — human neighborhood; simple needs, meals, lights, practical aid and voluntary community quests. The warm social center.
- **THE FAULT-LINE DISTRICT** — streets whose records disagree; multiple witnesses and versions coexist. Mystery area and stealth/negotiation.
- **CHAOS MEDIC LAB** — THE BATTERIES + WALTER + BABY-DOLL plotline; failed patches, joyful absurdity, a serious withheld record.
- **THE RUNAWAY TRACE** — ruined archives, shadow-corridors, anonymity and consent decisions; puzzle exploration.
- **THE BLUE NETWORK** — BLUE WITCH signal paths and DOGS FROM UNIVERSE shortcuts; comic wrong destinations. A historical running gag has the crew trying to reach MADIUS CITY but repeatedly ending up in BEKASI; preserve as an optional comic mission, not geographic evidence.
- **THE ELECTRIC FOREST** — proposed location derived from an existing persona name, with electric visual language and environmentally sourced side stories clearly separated from fantasy.
- **THE CONSTELLATION** — not a throne or final court, but a navigable map of *reviewed relationships, missing context and alternatives*. It is the game's visible record of work.
- **AETHERION** — optional later fantasy realm structure: **10 realms / 100 levels**; not the existing WINGS 10 departments, spiritual attainment or a claim about the afterlife. Do not require all 100 levels for story completion.

These are *new game location proposals*. Existing original person identities, geographic events, scripture sources and historical archives retain their own canonical names and evidence.

## 8. Eight ACTS / ERAS — preserve original exact era names

### ERA 00 — BEFORE LIGHT
**Theme:** absence is not always emptiness.  
**Dramatic beat:** a fragmented prologue: a faceless figure performs a dance in an unlit expanse; the archive remembers a presence but cannot publish a name. A voice asks to be forgotten. The game declines to solve the request yet.  
**Goal:** curiosity and respect, not exposition dump.  
**Proposed reveal:** the first missing record may be a deliberate boundary rather than an accident.

### ERA 01 — THE ARRIVAL
**Theme:** enter, listen, observe.  
**Dramatic beat:** THE LISTENER arrives at the Signal Station. Static, an old clock and a partial message remain; the radio still plays. A LAH / THE BATTERY bursts in, declaring the crisis is a power problem. The first temptation is to repair all records automatically.  
**Gameplay:** one small walkable station, inspect clock, tune three channels, restore a generator, meet a crew NPC.  
**Reversal:** electricity restores the signal but not its missing meaning.

### ERA 02 — THE WITNESS
**Theme:** a record is not the whole truth.  
**Dramatic beat:** THE LISTENER visits people whose memory of one event differs. **Tick-Tock**, **MOONRIDLEY** (morning-framed puzzle), **Lost the Signal**, **Thunderstruck** and **The Breath** appear as *source-attributed story cards*, not unredacted private accounts.  
**Gameplay:** compare statements, track when something occurred versus when it was recorded; mark observed, reported, interpreted, unknown.  
**Reversal:** a perfectly filled-in answer can be less trustworthy than an honest blank.

### ERA 03 — THE RUNAWAYS
**Theme:** names, masks, loyalty, escape.  
**Dramatic beat:** THE SHADOW starts leaving trace paths; DOGS FROM UNIVERSE tells impossible stories; BLUE WITCH opens colorful unreliable routes; the party repeatedly misses MADIUS CITY. Comedy and pursuit carry the chapter, but the question deepens: *who is allowed to expose a runaway?*  
**Gameplay:** party quests, cross-map travel, relationship choices and puzzles.  
**Reversal:** an alias is not sufficient to prove identity; witness extraction without consent can be wrong.

### ERA 04 — THE EXPOSURE
**Theme:** making hidden things visible has consequences.  
**Dramatic beat:** THE BATTERIES' CHAOS MEDIC lab is responsible for a flawed archive patch. WALTER-WHITER believed the change would heal. BABY-DOLL is suspected of withholding the crucial record. NPD TEAM 24:27, 24:37 and 25:50 are **separate archival media/scene identifiers**, not arbitrary time-travel clues.  
**Gameplay:** repair corrupted links, witness multiple accounts, discover the motive of THE PERFECT RECORD.  
**Reversal:** the system's “cleanest” entry was manufactured; BABY-DOLL did not necessarily do wrong by declining exposure.

### ERA 05 — THE CHANGE
**Theme:** correction must be humane.  
**Dramatic beat:** THE LISTENER reaches THE FORGOTTEN / HAVEN. An ordinary person requires help while legendary characters debate what the archive should contain. The two **distinct** ZILL paths offer different kinds of reflection. The strongest choice is not to score who is holier; it is to provide practical assistance, invite accountable correction and preserve legitimate privacy.  
**Gameplay:** restore a workshop, deliver aid, teach a skill, review/annotate, allow withdrawal.  
**Reversal:** the most meaningful reward comes from helping someone who was never famous.

### ERA 06 — END OF AN ERA
**Theme:** loss, consent, accountability.  
**Dramatic beat:** the Constellation offers a false simple ending: publish everything, delete everything or keep only a perfect narrative. THE SHADOW appears at the edge of the completed signal. BABY-DOLL's history challenges the player's desire for closure.  
**Gameplay:** audit impactful actions, choose which verified and authorized records can be public, which remain private, which must show uncertainty, which should be corrected or withdrawn.  
**Reversal:** not knowing every identity is not failure. The best conclusion honors boundaries and consequences.

### ERA 07 — NEXT PHASE
**Theme:** help received becomes help shared.  
**Dramatic beat:** the restored signal is not a proclamation that the mystery is solved. It is an invitation to continue listening and helping. The Basecamp becomes a living city; communities support new journeys; other stories remain open.  
**Gameplay:** replayable idle restoration, episodic sidequests, voluntary shared projects, co-op exploration and social hub.  
**Ending line:** **“THE MOON DOES NOT HAVE TO ANSWER EVERYTHING.”**

The exact **eight era names/order** are directly recovered from MOONWITNESS II. **All scene plots, protagonists, locations and choice structures introduced above are proposed game adaptation, not assertions that those scenes were authored in the originals.**

# PART IV — HOW TO MAKE IT PLAYABLE WITHOUT LOSING THE STORY

## 9. Core gameplay loop

`HEAR A SIGNAL → EXPLORE → WITNESS → CHECK CONTEXT → HELP SOMEONE → REPAIR A BOUNDED SYSTEM → RECORD CONSEQUENCES → UNLOCK THE NEXT STORY`

- **RPG**: exploration, puzzle, dialogue, relationships, small co-op missions; no mandatory combat against scripture characters or contemporary groups.
- **Idle**: restore practical infrastructure in-fiction — radio repeaters, workshops, libraries, community kitchens and logistics. Resource production is *fictional game mechanics*, not miraculous physical energy. The system continues producing bounded resources according to server-verified timestamps; the player does not need to grind to unlock compassion.
- **Offline**: saved exploration, downloaded lore, personal noncompetitive restoration; actions wait in an outbox. Offline claims about rare competitive items/trades are not authoritative before online reconciliation.
- **Online**: voluntary shared Haven, co-op world tasks, opt-in guilds and community discovery. No forced public exposure of private witness documents, no religious rank/karma tracking.
- **Fighting**: optional stylized mechanical disturbances, puzzles, non-human fabricated hazards or combat encounters in clearly fictional AETHERION; core story can advance through nonviolent choices.

### Proposed in-game resource vocabulary

| Resource | Meaning in fiction | Why it exists | Never means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signal | Power/coverage for messages | Exploration, radio, map unlocking | Divine revelation |
| Parts | Physical repair materials | Idle workshops/crafting | Real-world energy generation |
| Time | Mission timers and scheduling | Offline/online pacing | Scriptural age or cosmic control |
| Notes | Player-collected lore fragments | Reasoning/puzzles | Proven facts without source |
| Trust | Local relationship state earned through actions | Character affinity | Real moral worth/faith/salvation |
| Care | Community-service capacity | Haven repair and social quests | A purchasable measure of compassion |

Do not use **ALLAH, Asmaul Husna, salvation, divine reward** as XP or in-game currency. Monetary commerce in the wider Empire must remain distinct from the fictional game economy.

## 10. Main character growth (ROCKSOUL Four Pillars)

Source reflection pillars: **INPUT → DIRECTION → INTENTION → OUTPUT**.

In gameplay, these become *decision lenses*, not religious rankings:

1. INPUT: What did the Listener actually perceive, and from which source?
2. DIRECTION: What course of action or investigation is chosen?
3. INTENTION: What did the character think they were trying to achieve?
4. OUTPUT: What happened, and who was affected?

The important mechanic is revisiting an earlier decision after learning more. Source versioning and the original **Past Presence → Audit → Evaluation → Balancing → Promoting** loop become fun narrative revisitation rather than a courtroom simulation.

## 11. The 33 World Patches and 99 archive chapters

The historic **33 World Patches** are a status-preserving source registry, not 33 divine approvals or 33 accomplished miracles. For the game, safely derive **fictional sidequest themes** such as Safe Digital World, Learn for Life, A Safe Place to Live, Care for the Forgotten and Earth Stewardship. A mission is completed only when its **in-game objective** is finished; it must never rewrite the source submission status. **Slot #014 remains an unresolved numbering conflict**, and #033 is a reported chat submission, not an externally approved act.

The **99 historical narrative chapters** are an archive index, **not** 99 authenticated complete original stories and not equal to the 99 Asmaul Husna, G.O.D. 99, 99 Human States or XP levels. The game can provide a *Library of the 99 Editorial Windows* that clearly indicates source-backed, derivative, redacted, unrecovered or unresolved status. No need to force all 99 into the first release.

**AETHERION** 10 realms and 100 levels form an optional fiction/game progression concept. They are distinct from organizational WINGS 10, AGE CODE, 8 eras and empirical human-age research.

## 12. Content taxonomy and authoring safeguards

Every scene/lore record should include at minimum `title`, `source_family`, `source_ref`, `canon_scope`, `original_or_adaptation`, `author_or_editor`, `characters`, `scene_order`, `occurrence_time_vs_recorded_time`, `confidence_or_review_state`, `rights`, `privacy`, `alternate_versions`, `release_status`.

Suggested **game editorial lanes**:
- **ORIGINAL_SOURCE:** actual cited original from a repo/manuscript; not silently edited.
- **HISTORICAL_SYNTHESIS:** reconstruction/summary grounded in prior materials; not verbatim author speech.
- **GAME_ADAPTATION:** new script, motives, locations and dialogue proposed specifically for the game.
- **FICTIONAL_LORE:** explicit invented lore within the game's world.
- **PERSONAL_WITNESS:** consent-controlled and private by default.
- **RESEARCH_CITATION:** exact edition/locator/rights and attributed interpretation.
- **UNKNOWN / CONFLICTED / WITHHELD:** valid states, not failures to be “auto-filled.”

Public/creative releases must keep factual claims, theological traditions, private testimony and game lore distinct. Do not replace an unresolved claim with AI invention; do not make a humorous voice line into a quote from ALLAH; do not place sacred figures as game enemies, romance avatars or boss fights. Document cultural and rights review for commercial music, lyrics, celebrity likeness, art and sacred-text translations.

# PART V — PROLOGUE SCRIPT (NEW, PROPOSED GAME ADAPTATION)

## Episode 00 — TICK / STATIC / HELLO?

**BLACK SCREEN.**

There is no music. There is the smallest sound of an old clock.

**TICK.**

Then nothing.

A line of pale blue pixels runs across a dark screen, almost forming the outline of a moon. Another line tries to follow it and breaks halfway. An unknown station emits a low hiss. One character in a hoodie sits on a metal stair. Their face is unseen.

**ARCHIVE [UNVERIFIED]:** A signal has been received.

**ARCHIVE [INCOMPLETE]:** Sender identity: [WITHHELD].

**ARCHIVE:** Should this record be repaired?

A bright voice interrupts the static.

**THE BATTERY:** “Sinyalnya mati? Ya sudah, kita cas! LESGO!”

A second character enters, carrying a toolkit so large it can barely pass the door.

**WALTER-WHITER:** “Saya dokter, bukan teknisi radio.”

**THE BATTERY:** “Kalau begitu, diagnosis dulu. Kenapa dia diam?”

**WALTER-WHITER:** “Karena... dia radio.”

A sharp electric-blue arrow flashes above a broken map.

**BLUE WITCH:** “Lambreta! Saya menemukan jalan pintas.”

**DOGS FROM UNIVERSE:** “Bagus! Ke Madius City?”

A destination card appears:

**DESTINATION CONFIRMED: BEKASI. AGAIN.**

Long silence. No one mentions it.

In the background, THE LISTENER picks up a damaged cassette label. Written on it is a question:

**WHAT DESERVES TO CONTINUE?**

A second handwritten sentence is barely visible:

**SOMEONE ASKED TO BE FORGOTTEN.**

The player is offered an apparent choice:

- **PLAY THE RECORD** — inspect what can lawfully be heard.
- **KEEP IT SEALED** — preserve the sealed artifact's existence without revealing its contents.
- **ASK WHY** — discover what is known about consent and source origin.

All three choices are valid openings; none confers automatic ownership of the private record.

**THE MOON APPEARS, SILENT.**

`ROCKSOUL: LOST SIGNAL`  
`A MOONWITNESS XV STORY`  
`ERA 01 — THE ARRIVAL`

## Episode 01 — ONE LIGHT AT HAVEN

For the first real quest, THE LISTENER is asked to restore one room's light for an anonymous resident at Haven. The repaired radio station receives a new fragment only after the player completes a modest act of care. No miraculous reward descends; the player sees that a repaired power line, a shared meal and a listened-to person are themselves meaningful progress. The crew may make jokes, but the ordinary character is not the punchline.

The restored signal says only: **“Thank you. You remembered the right thing.”**

This does not resolve THE SHADOW's identity. It establishes the game's ethics in play.

## 18. Specific gameplay implications and content migration gate

### Source IDs and game IDs

A `game:episode` is never equal to a historical `source:passage` or a real `person`. All canonical source ownership stays outside this repo unless a separately approved, version-pinned and rights-cleared materialized derivative is explicitly imported. Game content stores `source_url`, `source_revision` (if inspected), `source_fragment_locator`, `claim_class`, `adaptation_transform`, `rights_state`, `editorial_state`, and `redaction_state`.

### Choice / memory / identity

Every central quest should allow these outcomes where meaningful: `DISCOVER`, `WITHHOLD`, `RECHECK`, `CORRECT`, `PRESERVE`, `RELEASE` and `UNKNOWN`. The game must NOT pay extra rewards for doxxing a protected witness or for inventing certainty.

### Narrative content test

A candidate episode passes editorial QA only when it has a player desire, obstacle, choice, consequence, character response, continuity link, accessible presentation, and source/fiction classification. All possible choices must avoid permanently exposing private identity in public/shareable data.

### Game ownership exception

The 2026-10-26 earlier instruction **"XV is the only forward writing repository"** applied before the owner opened a game-specific repository. The owner's newer explicit decision is **game development in `bjo163/rocksoul-game`**; the XV broader archive/technology remains the reference source. Do not copy XV's full codebase, hub, Corpus, personal records or secret configurations to this repo.

The strongest fiction premise is not "How do we restore everything?" but "**Which things have we earned the right to preserve, and which things should remain private?**" THE SHADOW's requested forgetting, BABY-DOLL's plot twist, THE BATTERY's impulse to patch, and THE FORGOTTEN's need to be seen each disagree in a way that makes the world playable.
