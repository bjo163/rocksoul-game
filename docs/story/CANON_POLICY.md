# ROCKSOUL GAME — Canon Constitution & Editorial Safeguards
**Status:** Foundation policy v1.0 · 2026-10-26 · Applies to `rocksoul-game` only. This is a working publication standard, not an assertion that all fictional ideas are ratified.

## 1 — Authority and content lanes
| Lane | Meaning | Example | Game use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SOURCE_RECORDED` | Verifiable statement contained in a named source/version | II era title, an alias in the original blueprint | Accurate attribution; no silent rewrite |
| `INTERPRETATION` | Authored reading, analysis, theology, semantic research | Comparative title or GOD-PROMISE framework | Optional, clearly identified lore/research |
| `PERSONAL_TESTIMONY` | Private or attributed personal recollection, not independently verified | Tick-Tock, dreams, intimate written stories | PRIVATE by default, no import without explicit consent |
| `CREATIVE_ORIGINAL` | Identified original authored art or fictional story with source | THE BATTERIES, NPD scripts and posters | Only after original version and usage rights check |
| `GAME_CANON_CANDIDATE` | New story/game adaptation created for this project | THE LISTENER, THE PERFECT RECORD, new dialogue | Writable in this repo, clearly marked proposed |
| `APPROVED_GAME_CANON` | Adaptation reviewed/accepted by human owner | Future signed-off quest script | May ship once asset/privacy/security checks pass |
| `UNKNOWN` / `CONFLICTED` | Missing evidence or incompatible versions | World Patch #014, four unrecovered BATTERIES books | Preserve ambiguity, block as historical source |

Do not promote `SOURCE_RECORDED` into empirically verified truth by default. An authored or religious source retains its native epistemic scope. **No AI author or software can canonize, reveal, or authorize religious truth.**

## 2 — Names that must never silently merge
- `ALLAH` is the Divine referent in religious contexts. `A-L-L-A-H` is a project acronym (Accountable Life, Learning & Action Hub); `THE BATTERY/A LAH/LiAlH4` is a fictional character/alias. None are identical.
- `ARSY` the XV tech hub is not the theological `al-'Arsh`. Existing XV radio and potential future Signal game sound are not automatic content/stream rights.
- `SHADOW` fictional character is not `SHADOW` creative/editor workspace or historical `legacy-taxonomy:shadow-root`.
- `ZILL — Shadow Witness` is not `ZILL — Ahluz Zikir/Tasawuf`; older `ZIIL` spelling stays source-attributed.
- `MYLUFT`, `MA'LŪF`, `HONEY`, `QUEEN R`, `Al-Wadūd` are not globally identical.
- `00-MAIN GOD PROMISE`, `GOD PROMISE: THE ROCKSOUL` and the research `GOD-PROMISE MOONWITNESS 1·6·3` remain separate originals.
- `G.O.D. 99`, 99 Asmaul Husna, 99 Human States, 99 V10 chapters, and 100 AETHERION levels are separate systems.
- AETHERION 10 realms ≠ organizational WINGS 10; four SOUL pillars ≠ Empire's four OPERATING pillars ≠ other 4-count traditions. II eight narrative eras ≠ historical AGE CODE ≠ calendar.

## 3 — Religion, spirituality, research
The game's stated artistic inspiration may involve devotion to ALLAH, care, correction, justice, reflection, sound, humor and researched traditions. However:
- Do not depict ALLAH as an NPC, boss, playable avatar, collectible, battery, currency, AI, human impersonator, or score giver.
- Do not assign salvation, miracle approval, prophetic status, divine election, faith level, spiritual superiority, or authority via RPG rank or leaderboard.
- Do not impersonate real prophets or animate them as mechanics of supernatural combat; source study belongs in source-attributed research content.
- Different Islamic, Christian, Jewish, Hindu, Buddhist and other textual/traditional readings must be attributed, not forcibly equated or mocked as factual verdicts.
- A joke assigned to a fictional character is *character dialogue*, not an authentic divine/scriptural quote. Preserve original scripture wording, translations, editions, rights, and attribution.
- `RGBL` philosophical framework and `SRGBL` research method are human-made systems, not proven cosmological or scientific laws.
- Science/cosmos/energy technologies in the game are fictional abstractions, not claims of actual quantum power, consciousness, immortality or supernatural intervention.

## 4 — Privacy, memory and character consent
- Private ChatGPT logs/diaries/case notes are `PRIVATE_DEFAULT`. They do not belong in public Git. The game may use a separately reviewed, materially anonymized creative adaptation.
- THE SHADOW's consent dilemma is **fictional narrative morality**, not a feature to expose private people. No actual personal data is collectible as loot.
- A witness may be remembered in a private fictionally-locked record without being publicly identifiable. The title THE SHADOW and historical aliases must not be cross-linked to real people without real evidence and permission.
- Distinguish event time, recorded time, publication date and game fictional time. Tick-Tock's recovered observed/replacement events are not proof that time supernaturally stopped.
- Revocation requests and licensing takedowns must be addressed in publication and import lifecycle.

## 5 — Copyright / derivative content
Historic references to Metallica, RHCP, MGMT, Kesha, Atreyu, Yellowcard, and other artists are metadata, **not rights to stream, replicate lyrics, or bundle commercial tracks**. Default to original music and sound effects, or duly licensed assets.
Original posters/author imagery and third-party portraits are not automatically public-domain. Character names that overlap celebrity names require new non-infringing design or explicit license.
Public repository visibility is not a license grant. Separate `SOURCE_LICENSE`, `DERIVATIVE_PERMISSION`, `MEDIA_USAGE` and `PUBLICATION_APPROVAL`.

## 6 — Canon promotion process
`DRAFT → SOURCE_LINKED → REVIEW_REQUESTED → APPROVED_GAME_CANON → RELEASED`.
Possible exits: `REJECTED`, `WITHHELD`, `RETRACTED`, `NEEDS_ORIGINAL`.
Promotion requires a human owner/reviewer, source refs, source revision, rights state, safe public metadata, a revision/decision note and tests for all affected published quests. AI-generated text remains labeled as adaptation.

## 8 — Narrative review tests
Every episode: coherent player goal, obstacle, cost/choice, emotional beat, downstream consequence, sources where adapted, optional unresolved outcome, no compulsory faith classification, no private identity disclosure, age-appropriate content and an accessible text alternative. A green test build does not replace these human editorial checks.
