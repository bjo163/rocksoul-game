# ROCKSOUL GAME — Visual & Audio Identity v1.0
**Status:** Art direction candidate, not a completed asset pack. The Moonframe 15-skin/30-appearance system and historical Rocksoul assets are inspiration, not shipped dependencies.

## Creative signature
**MOON + LINES + STATIC + HEART + CHAOS.** Story-first, youthful and experimental; mysterious and dark without turning it into a sterile enterprise dashboard or an inaccessible particle shower. Historical reference: *Lines — Visual Effect Mansa*, manga speed lines, brutalistic typography, photocopy paper/grain and playful, respectful comics.

## Four visual families
| Story layer | Composition language | Preferred motifs |
|---|---|---|
| WITNESS | Negative space, silent horizon, lunar orbit, fragment trails | monochrome/navy with restrained electric blue |
| ROCKSOUL / HEART | Intimate close framing, light falling on objects, paper note/memory | warm gold, muted silver, deep indigo |
| THE BATTERIES / CHAOS | skewed comic panels, high-impact type, jump-cuts/scratch FX | yellow, magenta, bold outlines |
| RESEARCH / JOURNEY | clear graph and archive cards, citations/uncertainty flags | readable UI on neutral surfaces with one accent |

## Character direction
Default concealed faces; hoodies/masks/back-facing silhouettes; identities communicated through gesture, shape, light and typography. THE SHADOW should not look inherently evil. THE BATTERY is funny but not a deity/light-being. No automatic use of real celebrity portraits, faith figures or private images.

## Required design deliverables
- Brand lockup/master logo candidates with vectors drawn or licensed; favicon/app icon; screenshots of light and dark themes.
- Player UI: HUD, story dialogue, choice sheet, source/uncertainty markers, inventory, generators, progress map, quest cards and social settings (later).
- 2D asset kit: scene tiles, props, beacon, radio, clock, safehouse, wayfinding and animation silhouettes.
- Audio: original title sting, static/radio pulse, tick/tock, interaction loop, ambient electric forest and a reduced-sound alternative. Commercial songs referenced historically are not included without rights.
- Optional skins can be distinct in color/texture but **must not change semantic behavior**, contrast, keyboard navigation or UI API unexpectedly.

## Accessibility gate
Text contrast and scaling, keyboard focus, reduced-motion, captions/transcripts, volume controls, color-independent status, dynamic font resizing, controller/touch parity where implemented. No narrative choices hidden behind audio or visual FX alone.

## Reference integrity
Review `bjo163/rocksoul-assets`, `bjo163/moonframe`, older MoonWitness image briefs and historical poster drafts before importing; tag each reused binary with source, author, license, derivative and approval. If unknown, do not ship.
